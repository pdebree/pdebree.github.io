## About Me

Hi! I am a Master's Student in Applied Statistics for Social Science Research at New York University. 

I am interested in methods for modeling latent traits across repeated measures, and designing survey that correspond to methods. My hope is to work on adapting sequence analysis and traditional psychometric methods to repeated surveys. I hold an Bachelor's of Arts from Boston University in Economics and Mathematics, with a Minor in Computer Science. 

Outside of work, I enjoy fiber crafts, logic puzzles and biking around the city!

## Research Interests

- Latent Variable Modeling
- Representation Learning
- Survey Design for Repeated Measures
- Psychometrics
- Bayesian Statistics
- Computational Social Science
